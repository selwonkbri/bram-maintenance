Loading

Pulling tasks, service log, and odometers.

How to read this. Overdue and Due Soon come from the Status formula in Notion: at or below zero days or miles is overdue, within 30 days or 1,000 miles is due soon.
Guidance lives in code, not Notion. Every item expands to show which lubricant it takes, what to avoid, the steps, and the specs. That content is in api/guidance.js, keyed to the task title. Rename a task in Notion and its guidance stops matching, and a warning appears at the top of this page.
No-due-date items are not shown. Every Trip, Before/After Storage, and As Needed tasks still exist in Notion but are filtered out here, because a board full of items that can never come due trains you to stop reading the board.
The baseline. Items marked BASELINE have never actually been serviced. The trailer arrived with no service history, so a clock-start entry dated 2025-07-15 gives every interval something to measure from. It is not a record of work performed.
Trailer mileage is an estimate of towed miles starting from zero, so lean on the calendar triggers rather than the mileage ones on the trailer.
Generator items show as never logged on purpose. The Champion 4000W was bought new and separately, so its clock does not start with the trailer.
